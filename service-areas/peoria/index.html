Peoria's commercial real estate is split across two distinct clusters. The P83 Entertainment District, anchored around the spring training complex at 83rd Avenue and Camelback, has dense retail, restaurant, and event-space facilities that generate significant foot-traffic residue — the kind that requires more frequent cleaning than a standard professional office. North of that, along the Happy Valley Road and Peoria Avenue corridors, the commercial stock is primarily professional offices, medical practices, and smaller retail tenants.

Peoria is roughly 8 miles from our Glendale base. Service runs from our established route through 75th Avenue northward, reaching the Lake Pleasant Parkway corridor without meaningful travel delay.

These typically require nightly cleaning with documented access procedures, given the 24-hour operations on neighboring docks.
