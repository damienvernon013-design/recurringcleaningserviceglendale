Commercial cleaning contracts for El Mirage offices, medical suites, and commercial facilities. About 13 miles from our Glendale base. Written scope, named crew, 22 years in business.

El Mirage has undergone notable commercial growth along the Dysart Road corridor between Bell Road and Thunderbird Road. New retail centers, professional office developments, and light industrial facilities have filled in what was largely residential a decade ago. The Dysart Road and Bell Road intersection in particular has become a meaningful commercial node serving the growing northwest Valley population.

El Mirage is about 13 miles from Glendale by the most direct route through the 75th Avenue or Dysart Road corridors. The drive time during morning hours — when nightly cleaning crews are finishing — is typically under 20 minutes.

The city's commercial zoning along the 303 Freeway frontage has attracted logistics and light manufacturing tenants, which have different recurring cleaning requirements than retail or professional office.
