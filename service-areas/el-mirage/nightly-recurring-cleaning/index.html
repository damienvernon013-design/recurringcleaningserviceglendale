Nightly Recurring Cleaning in El Mirage, AZ

Recurring cleaning contracts for El Mirage commercial facilities. Written scope, named crew, 22 years in business.

A 14,000 sq ft light manufacturing and office hybrid facility near the Loop 303 corridor in El Mirage — 3,000 sq ft of office space with four private offices and a conference room, 11,000 sq ft of production and assembly floor — represents the mixed-use cleaning requirement common in El Mirage's industrial-zoned districts. The office portion requires standard nightly office cleaning; the production floor requires dust mopping, spill response areas, and restroom servicing for shift workers.

Mixed office-and-production contracts are scoped in two sections: the office scope and the production scope. They are priced separately because the labor requirements, chemical requirements, and time requirements differ substantially. The written contract makes this separation explicit so there is no ambiguity about what is covered in each area.

Shift-work facilities in the El Mirage industrial corridor often need cleaning during the transition between first and second shifts — typically early evening — rather than in the traditional overnight window. We document the service window in the contract and staff the route accordingly.
