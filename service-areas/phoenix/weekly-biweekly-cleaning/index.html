Weekly & Bi-Weekly Cleaning in Phoenix, AZ

Recurring cleaning contracts for Phoenix commercial facilities. Written scope, named crew, 22 years in business.

A 7,500 sq ft law firm on the Central Avenue corridor in Phoenix — partner and associate offices, a large conference room used for depositions, a document room, a break room, and three restrooms — is a common weekly contract in central Phoenix's professional district. Law firms have document-security requirements that affect cleaning: crews cannot move papers or open drawers, and any items left on conference tables during a cleaning visit are left exactly as found. This is documented in the scope as a specific constraint, not a general instruction to "be careful."

Central Phoenix's summer heat drives an operational rhythm where many businesses run early hours — starting at 7:00am or earlier to avoid peak heat. Weekly cleaning for early-open facilities is scheduled for the evening preceding the scheduled day, not early morning. This is confirmed in the contract rather than coordinated informally.

The Central Avenue medical and professional district has a high density of law firms, financial offices, and medical practices with shared building infrastructure — shared lobbies, shared elevators, shared restrooms on some floors. The scope documents which areas are the tenant's responsibility and which are the building's responsibility, so there is no ambiguity when both the tenant and the building management have cleaning service.
