Nightly Recurring Cleaning in Phoenix, AZ

Recurring cleaning contracts for Phoenix commercial facilities. Written scope, named crew, 22 years in business.

A 19,000 sq ft insurance company headquarters in west Phoenix — open-plan call center floor with 60 workstations, management offices on the perimeter, a large break room used by multiple shifts, an IT server room lobby, and four restrooms — requires nightly cleaning five nights per week. Call centers are among the highest-contamination office environments per square foot: shared headsets, keyboards, and workstations used by multiple shifts create surface-contamination accumulation that a weekly schedule cannot manage.

The break room in a multi-shift call center sees use throughout the day and across shifts. Nightly break room cleaning is not optional for a facility with 60 workstations across two shifts — it is a sanitation requirement. The restrooms serving that population volume require the same treatment.

West Phoenix call center facilities along the I-17 corridor are within the 25-mile Glendale service radius. Nightly contracts for 19,000 sq ft facilities at this density are scoped with two crew members minimum, with cleaning time documented in the contract to confirm the service window matches the facility's operational schedule.
