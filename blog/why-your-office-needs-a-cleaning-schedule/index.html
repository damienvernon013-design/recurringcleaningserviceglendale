Why Your Office Needs a Professional Commercial Cleaning Schedule

Glendale Recurring Cleaning | 22 years in the market

A cleaning schedule turns cleaning from a reactive task into a managed system. Here's how to think about building one that actually matches how your building is used.

What Is a Commercial Cleaning Schedule?

A documented plan specifying which tasks are performed, how often, and by whom — daily, weekly, monthly, and quarterly.

Why Consistency Matters More Than Intensity

Research on workplace hygiene consistently shows regular, moderate cleaning is more effective at controlling bacterial load than infrequent intensive cleaning.

Building Your Schedule by Frequency

Daily: trash and recycling, restroom sanitization, kitchen wipe-down, high-traffic vacuuming, spot-cleaning glass.

Weekly: full vacuuming, mopping, dusting, interior windows and partitions.

Monthly/Quarterly: deep restroom scrub, floor buffing or waxing, vent cleaning, carpet extraction, exterior windows.

Working With Your Provider

A good provider helps you build a schedule based on foot traffic, surface types, and industry requirements — this is exactly the kind of planning we walk through during our onboarding process.
