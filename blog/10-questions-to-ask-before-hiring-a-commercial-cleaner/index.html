10 Questions to Ask Before Hiring a Commercial Cleaning Company

Glendale Recurring Cleaning | 22 years in the market

Asking the right questions upfront saves you from problems that are much harder to fix once a contract is signed. Here are ten worth asking every provider you're considering.

  1. How long have you been in business, and can you provide references from similar businesses?
  2. Are your employees background-checked?
  3. Are you bonded and insured — can I see a current certificate of insurance covering general liability and workers' compensation?
  4. What products and equipment do you use — disinfectants, floor machines, HEPA filters?
  5. Who actually cleans my space — W-2 employees or subcontractors?
  6. What happens if I'm not satisfied with a clean — what's your complaint resolution and re-clean policy?
  7. Will you provide a written scope of work?
  8. What are your contract terms and cancellation policy?
  9. Do you have experience in my industry specifically?
  10. How do you train and supervise your staff?

A provider who answers all ten clearly and in writing — including proof of insurance and bonding — is generally a safer choice than one who hesitates on any of them.
