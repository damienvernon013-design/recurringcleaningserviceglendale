What to Look for in a Commercial Cleaning Contract

Glendale Recurring Cleaning | 22 years in the market

A commercial cleaning contract should leave no ambiguity about what you're getting, how often, and what happens if something goes wrong. Here are the sections that matter most.

Essential Sections

  • Scope of Work — itemized, not vague
  • Service Frequency and Schedule — specific days and time windows
  • Pricing and Payment Terms — watch for price escalation clauses
  • Contract Duration and Renewal — mark auto-renewal notice windows on your calendar
  • Termination Clause — notice period and any early-termination fees
  • Complaint and Remedy Process — how issues get reported and resolved
  • Insurance and Liability — policy limits and whether you're named as an additional insured

What to Negotiate

A 60-day trial period, mutual termination rights, and performance benchmarks tied to complaint resolution are all reasonable things to ask for before signing.
