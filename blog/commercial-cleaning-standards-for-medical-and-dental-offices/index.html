Commercial Cleaning Standards for Medical & Dental Offices

Glendale Recurring Cleaning | 22 years in the market

Medical and dental facilities need a different level of cleaning than a standard office. Here's what separates clinical-level service from general commercial cleaning.

Why Standard Commercial Cleaning Isn't Enough

Medical environments require clinical-level disinfection, not just cleaning — different products, dwell times, and bloodborne pathogen protocol knowledge.

Key Regulatory Standards

OSHA's Bloodborne Pathogens Standard (29 CFR 1910.1030), CDC Environmental Infection Control Guidelines, HIPAA-aware handling of documents and areas, and state dental board or health department standards, which vary by state.

What Medical Cleaning Includes That Standard Cleaning Doesn't

EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants used with proper dwell times, biohazard waste handling protocols, awareness of sharps container management, clean/dirty zone distinction, frequent patient-contact surface disinfection, and color-coded microfiber cloths to prevent cross-contamination.

Questions to Ask a Medical Cleaning Provider

Do staff have bloodborne pathogen training? Which EPA-registered disinfectants are used? Does the provider have experience with HIPAA-compliant procedures? Can they provide documentation for compliance files? Are staff background-checked?

For medical and dental offices along the Banner Boswell corridor and throughout the West Valley, these standards aren't optional extras — they're the baseline. Our insurance and bonding coverage is structured to support facilities with these requirements.
