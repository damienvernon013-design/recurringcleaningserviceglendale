Top 10 Areas Most Businesses Miss During Office Cleaning

Glendale Recurring Cleaning | 22 years in the market

Even a regular cleaning routine can leave gaps. These are the areas that most often get skipped without an explicit checklist calling them out.

  1. Keyboard and mouse surfaces — often skipped without explicit instructions; should be disinfected weekly.
  2. Light switches and outlet plates — high-touch, rarely wiped in standard routines.
  3. Elevator buttons and door push plates — high pathogen transfer points.
  4. Tops of filing cabinets and tall shelving — out of sight, out of mind.
  5. Refrigerator handles and microwave buttons — exteriors get wiped, but seals and handles are often missed.
  6. Air vents and return grilles — should be wiped monthly, deep-cleaned quarterly.
  7. Chair bases and casters — pick up debris and redistribute it; clean monthly.
  8. Phone handsets and headsets — a significant germ vector, rarely on standard checklists.
  9. Window blinds and tracks — accumulate dust rapidly, especially in dry climates.
  10. Under and behind furniture — dust and debris accumulate without periodic furniture-moving.

A written, itemized cleaning checklist is the most reliable way to make sure these areas don't fall through the cracks.
