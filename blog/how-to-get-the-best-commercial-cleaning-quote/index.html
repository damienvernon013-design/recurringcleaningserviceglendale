How to Get the Best Commercial Cleaning Quote Without Overpaying

Glendale Recurring Cleaning | 22 years in the market

Getting a good commercial cleaning quote takes preparation. A little groundwork before you contact providers makes the difference between an accurate quote and a rough guess that changes once the crew shows up.

Know Your Square Footage

Cleaners price by the foot — the more accurate your number, the more accurate your quote. Pull your lease documentation or measure the space directly rather than estimating.

Define Your Scope Before the Walkthrough

Write down exactly what you need before inviting providers in for a quote — number of restrooms, floor types, break rooms, and any areas that require special handling.

Get at Least Three Quotes

The spread between the lowest and highest bids for identical work is often 30–50%. Middle-range quotes are usually the sweet spot — the lowest bid frequently means a shorter task list or less-experienced staff.

Ask What's NOT Included

Always ask providers to tell you what falls outside their standard quote. This is the single most common source of billing surprises later.

Request References from Similar Businesses

A cleaning company that excels at retail spaces may not be the right fit for a dental clinic. Ask for references from businesses similar to yours in size and industry.

Negotiate Strategically

Offer a longer contract for a locked-in rate; ask if paying quarterly or annually reduces monthly cost; bundle services for a package discount.
