Recurring Cleaning Service FAQs for Glendale Businesses

Answers to the questions we receive most often before a quote and during the first month of a contract.

How do I get a price without a lengthy sales process?
Call (866) 958-8773 with your square footage, number of restrooms, and preferred frequency. We give you a rough range on the call and follow up with a written quote after a brief walkthrough. The initial conversation is under 10 minutes.
What is included in a recurring cleaning contract?
Everything that is in the written scope — which is produced per building, not from a template. Standard inclusions are vacuuming, hard-floor mopping, restroom sanitation, breakroom cleaning, surface dusting, and trash removal. Window cleaning, floor stripping, carpet extraction, and specialty disinfection protocols are scoped and priced separately.
Do you clean residential properties?
No. Our contracts are for commercial and professional facilities only: offices, medical and dental practices, retail spaces, industrial buildings, and multi-tenant commercial properties.
Can I pause or cancel the contract?
Pause and cancellation terms are written into the contract before you sign. We do not have a standard policy that overrides whatever you agree to — the contract document is what governs. Most contracts include a notice period of 30 days for cancellation. See our article on pausing or cancelling a recurring clean for a plain-language explanation of what to look for in a contract.
How quickly can service start?
After the walkthrough and signed contract, most contracts can start within one to two weeks. Nightly contracts that require background checks or site-access credentials may take longer. We will give you a specific start date when the contract is signed.
What if something is missed or not done correctly?
Email ops@thequotemasters.com or call (866) 958-8773 and reference the task in the written scope. Nightly-contract issues are addressed within one business day. Weekly and bi-weekly issues are corrected on the next scheduled visit unless the situation requires faster action.
Do you provide cleaning supplies and equipment?
Yes. Crews bring all supplies and equipment unless the facility has specific product requirements (certain healthcare settings, for example, require the use of facility-approved disinfectants). Any such requirements are documented in the scope.
Are you insured?
Yes. We carry general liability insurance appropriate for commercial cleaning operations, and every crew we dispatch is bonded. An insurance certificate is included with every written quote. Full details are on the insured and bonded page.
