What to Expect: Your First 30 Days in Glendale

From first contact to established routine — the steps, the documents, and the commitments involved in starting a recurring cleaning contract.

Step 1: Contact and initial conversation

When you call (866) 958-8773 or submit a quote request, someone from our team responds by the next business day. The initial conversation covers your facility type, approximate size, current cleaning situation, and the frequency you are considering. This takes about 10 minutes and is not a sales call — it is qualification. If your facility is outside our service area or your requirements do not match our capabilities, we will say so.

Step 2: Walkthrough and scope development

We schedule a walkthrough at your facility at a time that works for you or your facilities contact. During the walkthrough we document every area that will be in scope: floor types and sizes, restroom count and configuration, breakroom and kitchen areas, any specialty surfaces or compliance-sensitive zones, access points, and any tasks that are specifically excluded.

The written scope document is produced within two business days of the walkthrough. It lists every task in the contract, the frequency for each task, and the standard each task is held to. You review the scope before signing anything.

Step 3: Crew assignment

We assign a specific crew to your building before the contract is signed. You receive the crew designation in writing. If you require background checks or site-specific credentials for access, this is documented in the contract.

Step 4: First clean and walkthrough sign-off

The first scheduled clean is followed by a brief walkthrough with your facilities contact or office manager. Any items in the scope that were missed, under-performed, or need adjustment are documented and corrected before the routine begins. We do not consider a contract operational until the sign-off walkthrough is complete.

Step 5: 30-day check-in

One month after service begins, we schedule a brief check-in — a phone call or an on-site visit, depending on the size of the contract. We review whether the frequency is right, whether the scope needs adjustment, and whether there are any recurring issues. Any changes are documented in writing as a scope amendment. There is no cost for reasonable scope adjustments in the first 30 days.

Ongoing: what you can expect

After the first 30 days, the contract runs on the agreed schedule. If something is missed or below the scope standard, notify us at ops@thequotemasters.com or call (866) 958-8773. We document reported issues and address them within one business day for nightly contracts, or on the next scheduled visit for weekly or bi-weekly contracts.
