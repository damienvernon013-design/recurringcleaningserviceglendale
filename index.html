What We Do

Scheduled Cleaning Contracts for Glendale Commercial Facilities

Most cleaning problems — missed spots, inconsistent crews, surprise invoices — come from contracts that never specified what "clean" actually means for your building. We start with a written walkthrough and a scope document that lists every task, every frequency, and the standard each one is held to. You sign the scope before we schedule the first clean.

The cleaning requirements for a 22,000 sq ft distribution dock are not the same as a dental suite on Camelback Road. We scope each contract individually.

How It Works

From First Call to First Clean

01

Walkthrough & written scope

We visit your facility, document the square footage, surface types, and frequency requirements, then deliver a written scope within two business days. No scope, no contract.

02

Crew assignment

A specific crew is assigned to your building before the contract is signed. You know who is showing up before the first clean.

03

First clean & sign-off

After the first scheduled clean we do a walkthrough with your facilities contact. Any gaps are corrected before the routine begins.

04

30-day check-in

One month in, we schedule a brief call or on-site review. Adjustments to scope or frequency are documented in writing.
